Marbles2

One puzzle, four platforms. Clear the board by matching touching same-colour marbles — pick the hardware that takes you back.

  • Game Boy

    Homebrew ROM · 1989 hardware

    A proper Game Boy build with a normal mode, a timed fast mode, seed-based runs and a worldwide high-score table. Play in an emulator or on real hardware.

    Open Player guide

  • ZX Spectrum Next

    Modern 8-bit revival · 256-colour palette

    Built for the Spectrum Next — the 2020s reimagining of the classic Speccy. Crisper palette, proper sound, world leaderboard. Runs in-browser via emulator.

    Open About this build

  • Mobile

    Early iPhone webapp · circa 2009

    A touch-first HTML build from the first wave of iPhone webapps. Open it on a phone for the intended feel, or play on desktop with a click.

    Open

  • Palm Pilot

    Palm OS PRC · 160×160 greyscale

    The original Marbles² from the PDA era, running here in a CloudpilotEmu WebAssembly Palm emulator. Tap-stylus puzzling at its finest.
